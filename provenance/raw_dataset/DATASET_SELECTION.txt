Final preservation decisions
============================

The preserved dataset contains only the experimental outputs used to support
the final thesis/journal evaluation.

E1:
- RR, PF and MR: 100 seeds at 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80,
  90 and 100 MHz.
- IA-FMR alpha_full: 100 seeds at the same 12 bandwidths.
- Baseline runs at 35 and 45 MHz are excluded.
- Derived temporal tables are excluded because they can be regenerated from
  the preserved raw outputs using the public analysis scripts.

E2:
- IA-FMR at 40 MHz for 3, 5 and 7 UEs: 100 seeds each.
- The 9-UE case is the same 40-MHz IA-FMR dataset preserved under E1 and is
  therefore not duplicated.

E3:
- RR, PF and MR are preserved from the final MIMO rank-1 campaign.
- IA-FMR is preserved only from the final alpha_full campaign.
- The earlier IA-FMR version present in the all-modes campaign is excluded.
- dl_harq_feedback CSV files are excluded because they were diagnostic outputs
  and are not required by the final public analysis artifact.

Training:
- The 12 final alpha_full training logs are preserved under
  provenance/training_logs/final_alpha_full/.
